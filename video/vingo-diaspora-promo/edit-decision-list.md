## Sound
- **Voice:** OpenAI `tts-1-hd`, voice "nova", 0.95× speed, generated by the n8n workflow **"Vingo Promo Video – Voiceover & Transcribe"** (Gateway credits). The lines are in `build/vo_lines.json`.
- **Music:** "Dreaming Big" by Ahjay Stelino, from Mixkit (`assets.mixkit.co/music/31`), [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree) (commercial use OK, no attribution). It starts 15s into the track so its peak lands on the shop/mela section. It is quiet under the hook, swells at "What if home…", ducks automatically under the voice, and dips for Nanu.
- **SFX:** Mixkit free sound effects (whoosh, magic, sparkle, pops, shop bell, shutter, crowd, chime), same licence.
- **Flow audio kept:** snow/room ambience, Riya's line, sparkle, mela crowd, Nanu's line. **Muted:** the committee clip (off-story dialogue) and the video-call wave (it said "Hi Lan! Hey Nicole!").

## Known limits / next improvements
1. **Screen recordings:** the Magic Alpona and AI Writer moments show the real website but not the real product doing the transformation. A 5-second screen recording of each feature from vingobd.com/create would make it even more convincing, and I can drop it straight in.
2. **The typing shot (0:32) is cropped about 2.4×**, so it is a little soft. Re-generating it in Flow with a blank laptop screen would fix that.
3. **The video-call clip (1:13)** has a different room and outfits from the other home shots. It's short enough to work, but a re-generation using REF_home, REF_ammu and REF_riya would match perfectly.
4. **Landing page:** vingobd.com is Bangla-only. US viewers who tap through would convert better on an English (or bilingual) landing page.

## Rebuild
```bash
cd build
python3 site_capture.py && python3 site_capture2.py   # website stills (needs webfetch.py)
python3 gfx.py                                        # captions, chips, animated segments
python3 build_video.py                                # final mp4s -> ../output/
```
The music and SFX aren't committed (the Mixkit licence doesn't allow redistribution). Re-download them into `build/music_candidates/31.mp3` and `build/sfx/*.mp3` from the Mixkit IDs above: whoosh 1489, pop 2358, magic 2350, sparkle 866, chime 1107, shutter 1133, bell 933, crowd 424.
