# Vingo diaspora promo: storyboard (60–90s)

Audience: Bangladeshi-Americans. Goal: free sign-ups at vingobd.com.

## 2. Which parts to focus on (the persuasion arc)

| Beat | Website section used | Why it works for diaspora in the USA |
|---|---|---|
| **HOOK** (0–8s) | Hero "আজ কী ডিজাইন করবেন?" plus festival emotion | Homesickness: "Eid in New York doesn't have to feel far from home." Canva has no alpona and no Bangla. That gap is the hook. |
| **2nd impression, the WOW** (8–25s) | **Magic Alpona** (hero feature) and the Heritage Art Library | The one feature no US tool has. A plain phone photo turns into a Nakshi Kantha festival card in one click, which is very visual. |
| **Value + context** (25–50s) | Bangla AI Writer, Smart Resize, Background Remover, 10,000+ templates | Answers "I can't write proper Bangla anymore" (second generation), plus one design fitting WhatsApp, Instagram and print. The template counts are proof. |
| **Community / connection** (50–65s) | Team collaboration, Vingo for Business, mobile app | Mosque and community-center event committees, Bangladeshi restaurants and grocery stores in Jackson Heights, wedding families spread across states. |
| **OFFER + CTA** (65–80s) | "Free to start, no credit card" | Low-risk ask. End on the brand line and URL. |

**Recurring connection thread:** one family, the **Rahman family in Queens**, appears in every beat (mom, a daughter who doesn't read Bangla well, dad who owns a shop). The viewer follows a story instead of a feature list.
