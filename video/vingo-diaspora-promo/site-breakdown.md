# Vingo (vingobd.com): site breakdown

Source: https://vingobd.com/ homepage, read on 2026-09-27. The site is Bangla-only; translations are ours.

**Brand**
- Name: **Vingo**, based in Dhaka. Its line: "a design platform made for Bangladesh's festivals and culture"
- Hero line: **"আজ কী ডিজাইন করবেন?"** ("What will you design today?")
- Sub-line: posters, cards and reels for every Bangladeshi festival, from Pohela Boishakh to Bijoy Dibos, **made with AI in minutes**
- Offer: **free to start, no credit card needed**

**AI tools (4 headline features)**
- **Magic Alpona**: wraps Nakshi Kantha and alpona patterns around any photo in one click
- **Background Remover**: removes the background in one click, for product photos or profile pictures
- **Bangla AI Writer**: writes captions, invitation text and poetry lines in Bangla
- **Smart Resize**: one design becomes a Facebook post, a banner, an invite and a print file

**Creative Suite**
- Brand kit, social posts, invitation cards and video in one workflow
- Event presentations and a social content calendar

**Templates: 10,000+**
- Wedding cards 510+ · Pohela Boishakh 420+ · Eid Mubarak 380+ · Birthday 330+
- Bijoy Dibos 290+ · Independence Day 260+ · Durga Puja 210+ · Ekushe February 180+

**Ecosystem**
- **Heritage Art Library**: Nakshi Kantha, rickshaw art and Jamdani motifs
- **Vingo for Business**: brand kits, menu cards and packaging design for small businesses
- **Mobile app**: "make a poster standing in your shop, write a caption sitting in a rickshaw"
- **Team collaboration**: real-time editing for event committees, small agencies and families

**What is missing from the site (this matters for the video)**
- No prices, no testimonials, no user counts, no English version, no mention of the USA or the diaspora.
- So the video cannot claim "X users" or quote a price. It sells **emotion, the heritage art, speed and "free"**.
- Recommended before launch: an English/Bangla toggle or an English landing page. Otherwise US viewers who tap the link land on a page in Bangla only.
