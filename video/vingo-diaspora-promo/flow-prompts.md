# Google Flow (Veo) prompts: Vingo diaspora promo

How to use:
1. In Flow, create the **Ingredients** below first (generate or upload a reference image for each). Then add the matching Ingredients to every shot that lists them.
2. Paste each shot prompt as-is. Every shot is 8 seconds, 16:9, and keeps its subject centered so it can be reframed to 9:16.
3. Generate 2–4 takes per shot and keep the best one. Test shots 1 and 3 first, and lock the look before doing the rest.
4. **Never let Flow render app screens or text.** Shots with a "SCREEN" note leave the laptop or phone screen blank or glowing. The real Vingo screen recording is composited on in the edit.

## Global style (append to every prompt)

> Cinematic commercial, shot on ARRI Alexa with 35mm anamorphic lenses. Warm, golden color grade with deep reds and marigold accents inspired by Bangladeshi festival colors. Soft natural skin tones, gentle film grain, shallow depth of field, smooth slow camera movement. Photorealistic. No on-screen text, no logos, no subtitles, no watermarks.

## Ingredients (characters and locations)

**AMMU (mother)**: Bangladeshi woman, about 48. Warm brown skin, kind eyes, black hair with a few greys in a loose bun. Wears a deep green cotton sari with a thin red border and a simple cardigan over it. Small gold nose stud.

**RIYA (daughter)**: Bangladeshi-American woman, about 21. Long dark wavy hair, casual NYU-style hoodie in cream, small gold hoop earrings. Expressive and modern.

**ABBU (father)**: Bangladeshi man, about 52. Trimmed salt-and-pepper beard, glasses, light blue button-up shirt with sleeves rolled up. Grocery shop owner.

**NANU (grandmother, Dhaka)**: Bangladeshi woman, about 75. White hair, white cotton sari with a pale blue border, reading glasses. Sits in a sunlit Dhaka home with a ceiling fan and green window grills.

**HOME**: Cozy Queens, NYC apartment living room at night. Warm lamp light, patterned Bangladeshi cushion covers, a framed Nakshi Kantha on the wall, a window showing a snowy Manhattan-skyline glow.

**SHOP**: Small Bangladeshi grocery in Jackson Heights, Queens. Shelves of spices, rice sacks, mango crates, a glass front window facing a busy 74th Street.
